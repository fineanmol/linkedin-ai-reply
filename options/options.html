Allow background profile reading
Used by deep welcome-message drafts (Connections) and the Content Calendar's friend-post scrape. Off by default.
⚠️ Read before turning this on. When enabled, the extension opens hidden LinkedIn tabs to read other people's profiles and recent activity (About, headline, recent posts). LinkedIn's User Agreement prohibits automated scraping, so this may put your account at risk, and every visit shows up to that person as a profile view. The text it reads is sent to your selected AI backend (cloud for Gemini/Claude). Leave it off unless you accept that risk.
